# Code of conduct

This is a small project. The rules are short.

## What is expected

- Be straight with people. Say what you mean, and say it plainly.
- Assume the other person is trying to help. Most disagreements here are about
  a number being right or wrong, not about a person.
- Keep criticism aimed at the code, the reading, or the decision. Not the author.
- If you are wrong, say so and move on. Nobody keeps score.

## What is not

Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, sustained disruption, or publishing
someone's private information. None of it is welcome, in issues, pull requests,
commit messages or anywhere else the project reaches.

## If something goes wrong

Report it privately to michaelhannafarag@gmail.com. Reports stay between you and
the maintainer.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, or
block accounts, and will say why when doing so.
